Patented Routing Logic

Sequential Multi-Shop Checkout

Need fresh bread from the bakery and hot Wazwan from a restaurant? Traditional apps force you to place two separate orders and pay double full delivery fees. Enything chains multiple nearby stops into a single optimized delivery run with incremental leg surcharges.

  • Single unified cart across multiple local stores

Zero Surge Pricing

Pure ₹20/km Distance Calculation

We believe in complete pricing honesty. Instead of opaque algorithms that hike delivery charges during peak hours or light rain, Enything uses an admin-regulated ₹20 per kilometer base distance calculation. 100% of fair delivery earnings go directly to support our riders.

  • Verified GPS-measured driving distance
  • No hidden platform surge multipliers
  • Complete fare breakdown shown prior to payment

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Want to Know

Got questions? Here is how Enything operates in Jammu & Kashmir.

Where is Enything currently operational?
Enything is headquartered in Bandipora, Jammu & Kashmir, and is actively serving customers, local restaurants, grocery markets, and pharmacies across the Bandipora district, with upcoming expansions across the Kashmir valley.
How does multi-shop ordering work?
With Enything’s multi-shop technology, you can add items from different nearby stores into a single cart. Instead of paying multiple full delivery charges, you only pay a fair distance surcharge for the physical route connecting the shops, saving you substantial delivery fees.
How is the delivery fee calculated?
Delivery charges are strictly transparent: exactly ₹20 per kilometer base distance rate measured accurately from the store to your delivery address. There are zero surge multipliers or artificial rain charges, and delivery earnings are passed on to empower local riders.
How can a local restaurant or merchant sell on Enything?
Simply download the Enything app from the App Store or Google Play, select "Seller" during onboarding, input your shop details, and upload your menu. Our onboarding team verifies your store within 24 hours so you can start accepting orders immediately.
How do delivery riders earn with Enything?
Riders earn guaranteed payouts calculated per kilometer driven, along with order incentives. Our app provides built-in GPS navigation and a real-time earnings dashboard where you can track every completed trip.
What payment options are available?
We support convenient Cash on Delivery (COD) as well as 100% secure online digital payments (UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Debit/Credit Cards, and Netbanking) powered by Razorpay.
